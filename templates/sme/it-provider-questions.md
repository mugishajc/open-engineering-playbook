# Questions for your IT provider

Ask these once a year, before the contract renews. Write the answers down and compare them with last year's. A good provider answers each one with a date, a number or a document. The reasons behind each question are in [for small and medium businesses](../../leaders/for-small-and-medium-businesses.md#questions-to-ask-your-it-provider-every-year).
