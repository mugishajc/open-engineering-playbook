## The first hour

| Minute | Do | Done at |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 10 | Unplug affected computers from the network and turn off Wi-Fi. Do not switch them off. Do not delete, wipe or reinstall anything: it is evidence. Call the IT contact. | |
| 10 to 20 | The person in charge starts a written log: time, what was seen, what was done. Call the insurer before hiring anyone else. | |
| 20 to 30 | Switch to the downtime procedure below. | |
| 30 to 45 | From a clean device, change the passwords for email and admin accounts. Check the backups are safe and disconnected. | |
| 45 to 60 | Agree who tells staff and customers, and what. Check whether a personal data breach must be reported, and by when. | |

Do not pay a ransom before you have taken legal advice and spoken to the insurer.
