# Staff AI use policy

One page for everyone who works here. It covers chat assistants, writing tools, transcription and translation tools, and AI features inside the software we already use. Engineers who write code follow the [code assistant policy](../docs/code-assistant-policy.md) as well.

Owner: [name]. Approved by: [name]. Date: [date]. Review: every 12 months, or when we add a tool.

## Tools you may use

| Tool | Approved for | Account to use |
|---|---|---|
| [tool name] | [drafting letters, summarising public documents] | [business account only, not a personal one] |

Any other AI tool needs approval from [name] first. A free personal account is not approved for work.

## Data that must never go into an AI tool

Unless the tool is listed above as approved for that data:

- patient, pupil, client or customer records, including names, contact details, case notes and photos;
- staff records: pay, health, performance, disciplinary matters;
- passwords, bank details, card numbers and admin screens;
- contracts, prices or plans that are not public.

If you are not sure, leave it out and ask [name].

## Every time you use one

1. Check every fact, figure, name and date before the output reaches a customer, patient, pupil or regulator. You are responsible for what you send.
2. Do not let an AI tool make a decision about a person (an appointment, a grade, a refund, a hire) without a member of staff reviewing it.
3. Tell the person when a message they receive was mostly written by AI, if they would expect to know.

## If something goes wrong

If you pasted data that should not have gone in, tell [name] the same day. Nobody is punished for reporting it quickly. It may be a personal data breach that must be reported within a legal time limit.
